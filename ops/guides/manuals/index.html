Manual 1 of 4

Admission office

You take students on, pass them desk to desk, admit them, and decide on continuing students who never came back.

New this year
  • The year opened before NACTVET's results came. Some continuing students are marked Provisional. Treat them like everyone else at your desk.
  • New menu item Admissions ▸ Did not report, for continuing students who never came.
  • The application now shows the student's passport photo, address, guarantor / sponsor and the schools they attended.

01Start at My desk

Sign in. EduTrack opens Admissions ▸ My desk.

  • This semester counts every application by desk.
  • On my desk lists only what is waiting on you. Press Work it to open one.

A first year who has no NACTVET number yet is held under a temporary number such as TEMP/2026/0001. Replace it when the authority issues the real one (step 03).

02Take a new student on

Open Admissions ▸ New application.

  1. Kind: under New to the college — First year · Level 4, Second year · Level 5 or Third year · Level 6 for someone joining from another college. Under Already a student here — Continuing or Readmission.
  2. Registration number: leave blank for a new student. EduTrack gives a temporary one.
  3. First name, Middle name, Last name: exactly as on the certificate (NHIF's sheets need them apart).
  4. Programme, then the Form Four index No. written like S0123/0045/2021. For a new student the Level fills itself from the year and is locked; for Continuing or Readmission choose it.
  5. Press Open the application.

Second and third year students are new students too: they go the same way as a first year and pay the one-time charges. The desks show them as Second year (new) — joins at NTA Level 5.

A new student goes through these desks, in order:

Admission (intake)→Records→Finance→Admission→Admitted

A continuing student starts at finance:

Finance→Records→Admission→Admitted

03Work an application

The application window shows, top to bottom:

  • Where it is and what the student owes. To register says paid or not paid.
  • Results and what they pay for: last semester's outcome, the bill, the modules.
  • Student details, kept by records, with the passport photo on the right. Check that the photo is the person in front of you.
  • The NACTVET number: type it when the authority issues it, then Save the number.
  • Documents handed in, and a note for the record.

Then choose one:

  • Clear this desk → passes it to the next desk. At the last desk it admits the student and gives the college ID.
  • Send back… returns it to an earlier desk, with a reason.
  • Refuse ends it, with a reason.

Admitting a student sets their first portal password: their last name in capitals (Rehema Mtui → MTUI). Tell them on the spot, and ask them to sign in and choose their own the same day — anybody who knows their last name could otherwise get in first. It works before their level's modules are set up.

Admitting a student puts them in their level's modules if the department has set them up. If not, they are linked automatically when the Head of Department creates the modules. Nobody adds students to modules by hand, and linking never bills: the year's bill is raised once, at the finance desk.

04Continuing students, and "Provisional"

When the Principal opened the year, every continuing student was sent to Finance automatically, at the level a pass takes them to.

Everyone whose results were not confirmed yet carries a Provisional tag. Admit them as usual. The examination office settles them when the results arrive, and EduTrack fixes their place by itself if they failed.

Only Level 6 students finishing the programme were not placed: there is no next level for them. If continuing students are missing from the desks after the year opened, press Send continuing students to finance on My desk.

05Did not report

Open Admissions ▸ Did not report and choose the semester. It lists continuing students whose application is still open.

You may decide three months after admission opened. For 2026/27 Semester 1 (opened 12 October 2026) that is from 12 January 2027. Before then the page shows the date it opens, and no buttons.

  • Still expected: they told you they are coming. Nothing changes; the date you checked is kept.
  • Did not report: EduTrack asks for a short note, then cancels the place and reverses its fees. The student's standing becomes Did not report.

A student marked Did not report may come back in any later year. They appear under Expected back.

06Transfer to another college

Open Transfers ▸ Transfer, type the student's NACTVET registration number (or college ID) and press Find. Their details and last results show, with this year's bill.

  • Nobody leaves owing. In semester 1, last academic year must be paid; in semester 2, semester 1. If they owe, EduTrack says how much and there is no button: they settle it at finance first.
  • Last year's fees are not in EduTrack. When it holds no bill for that period, a box asks for finance's confirmation that nothing is owed: who said so and the reference (for example Accountant, 2025/26 ledger, 27 Oct). It is kept on the record.
  • Fill in the college they are going to, the date, the letter's reference and, if you like, the reason. Press Approve transfer.

Approved, their portal closes, their place from this semester on is cancelled and the fees from this semester on are reversed to nothing (in semester 1, one-time charges too), so the year's totals no longer count them. Money they paid for it stays on their account as credit; a refund is the accountant's. The Principal, DPARC and the accountant are told. Should they come back, it is as a new student.

Transfers ▸ Transferred records lists every transfer: where to, when, the fees reversed and the money held. View shows their details and last results as they stood that day. DPARC, records and the accountant can read it from the same menu.

Manual 2 of 4

Examination officer

You upload NACTVET's grades, prepare the semester review, and watch over the students placed while their results were out.

New this year
  • Semester Review ▸ Provisional places: who went on without results, and what their results say once uploaded.
  • You set the cut-off: the date by which the late results must be in.
  • A late result always belongs to the semester it was sat in. A student who failed 2025/26 Semester 2 is a 2025/26 repeater, never a 2026/27 failure.
  • Students see a semester's results only when you press Show to students in Results ▸ Results Summary.
  • You settle the provisional students yourself: passes, repeats and FAILs. They are told once you show the results.

01See who is provisional

Open Semester Review ▸ Provisional places. Each row shows:

  • Results of: the semester the missing results belong to, for example NTA Level 4 · Sem 2 · 2025/2026.
  • Placed in: where the student was put, for example NTA Level 5, Sem 1 · 2026/2027.
  • Results now say: Results not out until you upload them, then the outcome they give.

02Set the cut-off

Under Cut-off, pick the date for the new semester and press Save. Choose a date before the end-of-semester exam registration.

After that date, the Principal, DPARC and you see a warning on the dashboard for every student still provisional.

Rule: a provisional student may attend and sit CATs, but is not eligible for the end-of-semester exam until settled. NACTVET would not count it.

03Upload the late results

When NACTVET's sheet arrives, open Results ▸ Authority Grades.

  1. Semester: the semester the exam was sat in, for example Sem 2 · 2025/2026. Not the semester running now.
  2. Class level, then Sheet: Continuing or Repeaters. Continuing lists only students who sit every module as first sittings; anybody missing a module is on Repeaters, with only the modules they sit left to fill. Module columns are in code order.
  3. Sitting: First sitting, or Supplementary sitting for a supplementary sheet.
  4. Download Level Template lists the students by name, A to Z. Copy NACTVET's grades in, then Upload Authority Grades.
  5. Read the message after the upload: any grade not saved is listed, usually because the student is not enrolled in that module that semester.
  6. A mistake in the sheet? With the same semester, level, sheet and sitting selected, press Clear uploaded grades…, give the reason, and upload the corrected sheet. What was cleared is kept on record. It works only while the results are hidden from students and before anybody is settled on them.

The Principal and DPARC are told that results for provisional students are in. Provisional places now shows what each one's results say.

04What happens to each student

Passed everythingYou, the Principal or DPARC press Settle the passes. The place is confirmed. Nothing else changes: no new bill, no new application.
A supplementary still to comeThe student stays provisional. Upload the supplementary sheet with Sitting = Supplementary. A pass then settles them as above.
Results not completeDPARC or the Principal decides: Hold until results takes them off the desk lists until settled (nobody can clear them meanwhile), Let through lets them start admission. Hold all whose results are not complete does them all. A pass settled lifts the hold by itself. At the desks their tag says what the results say: results: PASS, REPEAT, FAIL or not complete.
Failed: repeat or discontinuedSettled one by one: press Confirm… on the row. The failure is recorded against 2025/26, the semester it was sat in. The 2026/27 place, its registration and its fees are called off. Marks earned in 2026/27 stay on record but do not count. The student then follows the normal repeat or readmission route.
Results still not out at the cut-offThe warning appears. Chase NACTVET. The student cannot sit the end-of-semester exam.

The semester review works as before: you prepare it with Read the results again, and the Principal or DPARC confirms. You settle provisional students as their results say. Overruling a result stays with the Principal or DPARC. Students are told about their place only once you show the results.

05Show the results to students

Uploaded results are hidden from students until you show them. Open Results ▸ Results Summary, pick the semester and the level. The bar at the top says Hidden from students or Shown to students.

  1. Withhold anyone who should not see theirs yet: tick them and press Withhold selected, or Withhold on one row. They see only "Withheld".
  2. Press Show to students. Each level is shown on its own.
  3. Changed your mind? Hide from students takes them off the portal again.

Students read every year under End of Semester, each semester under its own year. A provisional student also reads a note at the top of the portal.

06Earlier years and repeat sheets

For students whose earlier year EduTrack never held. Open Results ▸ Earlier Results and upload a class's sheets in this order:

  1. First sitting: the year on NACTVET's sheet (for example 2024/2025), the semester, the level, and the file. Its Modules sheet carries each module's credits.
  2. Supplementary, if the class had one.
  3. Repeat sitting: NACTVET prints it under the student's first year (exam numbers with MTS2). Choose the semester it was sat in, for example Sem 2 · 2025/2026.

Where NACTVET reprinted the old grade unchanged in red, the student did not re-sit: type NS. The module stays owed and the remark is REPEAT. A changed grade, or a green cell, was sat. Students see the repeat under the year it clears: "PST04211 — C (Repeat, Sem 2 · 2025/2026)".

A grade already in is never replaced by an upload. A different grade is listed and not saved: clear the sheet, or correct the one grade.

07Correct a grade

NACTVET re-issued a grade, a repeat was not sat, or a grade went into the wrong module's column? Open Results ▸ Corrections: the semester, the registration number, Find their grades, then the module and the correct grade (or NS), or tick Remove the grade when the student did not sit that module. Say why, add NACTVET's reference, and press Correct the grade.

It changes at once; DPARC and the Principal are told. The old grade is kept with the correction. A module with no result reads INCOMPLETE for the student.

Was the student already confirmed on those results? Open Semester Review, Show: Results now differ from the decision, and press Confirm again (provisional while a result is missing). Repeats the wrong decision raised are cancelled; a place it took away stays off — the student comes back through Admissions.

Manual 3 of 4

Accountant

You hold the finance desk of admissions, record what students pay, and clear them for registration and exams.

New this year
  • Provisional students are billed the full fees of the level they were placed in, like everyone else.
  • If their late results show a failure, EduTrack reverses that level's fees by itself. Money already paid stays as credit.
  • No refund when a student finishes after Level 5. Did not report reverses the fees of the place.

01Your desk

Open Admissions ▸ My desk. Every application waiting at Finance is listed with what the student owes. A Provisional tag marks a student placed before their results came.

Press Work it to open one.

02Clear a student for registration

  1. Where they will live: a first year must be marked Day or Hostel first. The bill depends on it.
  2. To register: not paid lists the charges that block registration and what is outstanding on them.
  3. Invoices, in the same window: press Tuition fee, Direct costs, the other fee, or Everything outstanding. The invoice opens ready to print with its reference; hand it to the student for the bank. No need to leave the application.
  4. When the slip is recorded (step 04) and it reads paid, press Clear this desk →.

A student the college has agreed to let through unpaid: add an override under Student accounts ▸ Waivers & Overrides. Required items (TPH book, calculator, rim paper, insurance) are debts and never block.

03A provisional student

The window shows Provisional beside the semester. The results line reads results not complete, placed provisionally.

Bill and clear them like anyone else at that level. You do not need to do anything when the results arrive:

  • Passed: nothing changes.
  • Failed: the placed level's fees are waived automatically, with an audit entry. What they paid is kept as credit for their repeat or return. The repeat charges are raised on the repeat route.

04Record a payment

Open Payments ▸ Record a Payment.

  1. Type the reference on the slip, for example BPH-40014, and press Look up.
  2. Check the student and what the invoice covers, then Record a payment against this invoice.
  3. Enter the amount received, the date on the slip, Paid through (CRDB bank deposit), the bank reference, the EFD receipt number and who paid.

Mobile money is not accepted. A slip paid in the holiday without an invoice: choose Paid into = the account on the slip. The same bank reference twice is stopped. Confirm only when one slip really pays for two students.

05Balances & Clearance

Student accounts ▸ Balances & Clearance lists what every student was billed, paid and owes, and what they are cleared for: Reg., CAT 1, CAT 2, Final, Results. Press Statement for one student's full account.

Department and programme: Finance Overview, Balances & Clearance, Collections and Executive Summary ▸ Finance each have a Department and a Programme filter — PST apart from the VETA courses, hair dressing apart from computer basics. Collections and the Executive Summary also say how much each department and programme brought in.

Finishing after Level 5No refund. After the Principal approves, the student is listed under Postpone & Finish ▸ Finishing: print the statement and clear them when nothing is owed.
Did not reportAdmission marks it. The fees of that place are reversed automatically.
One bill a yearThe year's bill is raised once, at your desk, when the student is admitted. Putting a student in a module never bills; the old Bill the students / Generate charges button is gone. Raised during the year: supplementary and special exams, repeat modules (200,000 each), a hostel place taken up later, the TPH book if missing.

Manual 4 of 4

Students: your portal

Your attendance, results, fees, requests and profile, on a phone or a computer.

New this year
  • Add your passport photo. It shows small on your dashboard and large on your profile.
  • Your profile now shows your address, your guarantor / sponsor and the schools you attended.
  • Level 5 students may ask to finish after Level 5.

01Sign in and read your dashboard

Open the college's EduTrack address. Type your NACTVET registration number or your college ID (e.g. BPH/PST/2026/014) and your password. Your first password is your last name in capital letters (Rehema Mtui → MTUI). Sign in on admission day and choose your own straight away.

The dashboard shows your photo by Welcome, your attendance, fee reminders with their due dates, and notices from the college. A coloured note at the top tells you anything you must act on.

02Add your passport photo

  1. Open My Profile ▸ Personal info.
  2. Press Upload photo (later: Change photo).
  3. Choose a recent passport-size photo: your face clear, a plain background, no cap or sunglasses.

A photo from your phone's camera is fine. The page makes it small before sending, so it uses little data. Only you and the college offices can see it.

Check Personal info, Academic info (schools before) and Next of kin. If something is wrong, take the correction to the records office.

03If you see "Provisional"

The note "Provisional — your place in NTA Level 5 depends on your NTA Level 4 results" means NACTVET has not released your last results yet, so you were placed in the next level early.

  • Attend, pay your fees and sit your CATs as normal.
  • You cannot sit the end-of-semester exam until your results confirm your place.
  • You passed: the note goes away and nothing else changes.
  • You have a supplementary: you sit it, and stay provisional until it is marked.
  • You failed: you repeat what you failed from last year. Your new place is cancelled and its fees are reversed. What you paid is kept for you.

04Finish after Level 5

Only for a student who has passed Level 5, owes no module, has no result pending, and is not yet registered for Level 6.

  1. Open Request a Service ▸ Finish after Level 5.
  2. Say why, if you wish, and press Ask the Principal.
  3. While it waits you can Withdraw it. The Principal approves or declines with a reason, and you get a notification.

Your award is NACTVET's Level 5 award. Fees already paid are not refunded. You may come back for Level 6 in any later year.

05Good to know

Forgot your passwordSee the ICT officer. Only the ICT officer and the Principal reset passwords.
No modules yetAdmitted, but your level's modules are not set up yet? You still sign in to your dashboard as usual — your fees, invoices, pass, profile and services all work. Your modules appear there by themselves when the department sets them up.
Semester passOpen What I owe ▸ My semester pass, print it at ID-card size, and take it to the admission office to be stamped. It is valid until the day before your next unpaid instalment — pay again and download a new one. Paid the whole year at once: a year. Not paid up: it says what is still owed, and you cannot sit that semester's exams until it is paid.
Signed out by itselfLeft without use for a while, the portal signs you out, to protect your account on a shared or lost phone. Sign in again.
You cannot come this semesterAsk to postpone under Request a Service, or tell the admission office. A continuing student who does not come for three months loses the place, and may come back in a later year.
Paying feesGenerate the invoice under Payments and pay at CRDB with its reference. Mobile money is not accepted. Bring the original slip to the accounts office.

Records office and the Principal

The steps of this year's changes that belong to records and to the Principal (or DPARC).

R1Records: the enrolment form and the photo

Open Students ▸ Find a student, search by name or number, press Open.

  • Upload passport photo… when the student has not added one. Any JPG or PNG; the page makes it 300 × 300 before sending.
  • From the paper Student Enrolment Form: Address, Guarantor / sponsor and phone, and Schools and colleges before (O-level, A-level, college: school, from and to years, index number).
  • Compulsory for every student — continuing and VETA too: a next of kin and Fees paid by (parents / guardian, a sponsor — then name the sponsor — or the student). Without them the records desk cannot be cleared.
  • For NHIF: Marital status, National ID (NIDA) and, for continuing students, the NHIF card No. Then Students ▸ NHIF sheets gives NHIF's New Students and Continuous Students sheets, filled from the records, ready to download; rows with something missing are marked.
  • A mistake? Correct it here and write Why the change. Every change is kept — what it was, what it became, who, when and why — under Changes to these details.
  • Press Save details. A wrong year is refused and nothing of that save is kept.

P1Principal / DPARC: open the year

Semester Review ▸ Review & advance, then advance. The preview tells you:

  • how many move up, and how many of them go provisionally;
  • who waits: only Level 6 students finishing the programme;
  • anyone who cannot be placed (no programme or level on record), with the reason.

One press moves every level and programme. Nobody has to be confirmed first: every review not confirmed goes on provisionally and is settled when the grades are in.

P2Principal: settle, and decide exits

Semester Review ▸ Provisional places: after the exam officer uploads the results, Settle the passes confirms every pass at once. Settle failures one by one.

Postpone & Finish ▸ Finishing after Level 5: Approve or Decline… with a reason. Approval finishes the student at Level 5, cancels their Level 6 application, and sends them to the finishing route. Only the Principal decides; DPARC can read the list.

Postpone & Finish ▸ Student status: the Principal records Suspended (until a date — the place and fees stay, no examinations, it ends by itself), Terminated (never admitted again) and Discontinued for cheating or misconduct (they may come back only as new). Results failures are now FAIL and are readmitted as before. DPARC is told of each.

Modules: tutors no longer pick their own. The Head of Department gives their department's modules to their department's staff (My department staff); DPARC and the Principal give any.
