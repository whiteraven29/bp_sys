## 1. How the VETA system is structured

| Item | Detail | Conf. |
|---|---|---|
| Awarding body | VETA awards the **National Vocational Award (NVA)**, which covers the first three levels of the TVET Qualifications Framework. | ✅ |
| Level I | Basic: the trainee performs routine tasks. | ✅ |
| Level II | Intermediate: routine tasks with added responsibilities. | ✅ |
| Level III | Advanced: non-routine tasks. | ✅ |
| Old names | Older job adverts and documents use **Trade Test Grade III / II / I** and "CBET Level I/II/III". These map to NVA Level I / II / III. | ✅ |
| Above Level III | Progression continues into NACTVET **NTA Level 4–6** (Basic Technician Certificate up to Ordinary Diploma). Some VETA centres also run NTA courses, with "local fees" of around TSh 400,000–600,000 per year. | ✅ |
| Entry to Level III | At least **two CSEE passes**. | ✅ |
| Course types | **Long courses** lead to NVA Level I–III, run over 1–3 years, and require an aptitude test. **Short courses** last from 1 day to 6 months and lead to a certificate of attendance or competence. **RPL** (Recognition of Prior Learning) assesses experienced workers. | ✅ / ⚠️ |

### 2.4 Laboratory Assistant

| Item | Detail | Conf. |
|---|---|---|
| Long course | Dar es Salaam RVTSC: **2-year long course, Level II**, TSh 60,000/year. | ✅ |
| Job market use | University lab-assistant adverts (engineering, civil, workshop labs) ask for **Form IV + CBET Level II / Trade Test Grade II from VETA**. | ✅ |
| **Important limitation** | **Health laboratory** posts ask for a **2-year Health Laboratory Sciences certificate**, a NACTVET / Ministry of Health qualification, not VETA. A VETA Laboratory Assistant certificate suits school, engineering and industrial labs, not clinical labs. | ✅ |

### 2.5 Veterinary / Animal Production

| Item | Detail | Conf. |
|---|---|---|
| What VETA offers | An **NVA in Animal Husbandry**: a practical vocational award (e.g. Kilacha runs VETA and NACTVET programmes). | ⚠️ |
| What VETA does not offer | **Animal Health and Production** certificates (NTA 4–5) and diplomas (NTA 6) are **NACTVET** programmes. Diploma entry needs A-Level science passes or an NTA 5 certificate. | ⚠️ |
| Practice regulation | Animal health paraprofessionals work within limits set by the **Tanzania Veterinary Council**. A VETA husbandry award is a production skill, not a licence to practise animal health. | ⚠️ |
| Duration and fees of the VETA NVA | Not found. | ❓ |

## 3. Academic year and intakes

### Long courses (NVA)

| Step | Timing | Conf. |
|---|---|---|
| Applications open | Official VETA document: **August**. A 2025/26 blog: VETMIS window **May to July/August**. | ✅ / ⚠️ |
| Aptitude test | **October**, in the region where the applicant applied. | ✅ |
| Selection results | **November or December**. | ✅ |
| Joining instructions | State the occupation and boarding or day. | ✅ |
| Training starts | **January** of the following year. | ✅ |
| Second intake | A **March intake** (window around February), one blog. | ⚠️ |
| How to apply | Online through **VETMIS** (`https://vetmis.veta.go.tz/`), up to 3 centres. | ⚠️ |
| Academic year | Roughly **January to November/December**, national assessment at the end. Follows the calendar year, not NACTVET's October–September. | ✅ (start) / inferred (end) |

### Short courses

| Item | Detail | Conf. |
|---|---|---|
| Intake | Rolling, set by each centre. Apply in person at the registrar. | ⚠️ |
| Application form | About **TSh 5,000**. | ⚠️ |
| Payment | Through a **GePG control number** only. | ⚠️ |
| Documents | NIDA ID or birth certificate, and 2 passport photos. | ⚠️ |

## 4. Assessment

| Item | Detail | Conf. |
|---|---|---|
| Approach | **Competence-Based Education and Training (CBET)**: assessed against occupational standards, not ranked. | ✅ |
| Structure | Units → modules → levels. A level certificate once the required units are achieved; units can be certified individually. | ✅ |
| National tests | At each of the three levels, verified by an external accredited assessor. | ✅ |
| Formative (in-centre) | Oral, written and product assessments, recorded in **logbooks**. | ✅ |
| National CBA sessions | **Twice a year: May/June and November/December**. | ⚠️ |
| Progression to Level II | Level I plus modules equal to 1 year of institutional training; or Level I + 3 years' work + modules equal to 2 years. | ⚠️ |
| Progression to Level III | Level II plus the required modules; or a lower certificate + at least 1 year of approved training, work experience and competence in the required modules. | ⚠️ |
| Exam centres and assessors | Registered VTCs can become VETA examination centres; their teachers join the assessor pool. | ✅ |
| CA vs national weighting | Not found. | ❓ |

## 8. To verify before relying on this

1. In a browser, check `https://vetmis.veta.go.tz/` and `https://www.veta.go.tz` for the **2027 intake window** and any March intake.
2. Call the nearest VETA RVTSC for current **short-course fees and dates**.
3. Ask the VETA zonal assessment office for the **current NVA grading scale** and the **CA / national weighting**.
4. Animal production: **VETA NVA Animal Husbandry** (Level I–III) or **NACTVET Animal Health & Production NTA 4–6**?
5. Lab assistant: **general / engineering labs** (VETA) or **health labs** (NACTVET health certificate)?
